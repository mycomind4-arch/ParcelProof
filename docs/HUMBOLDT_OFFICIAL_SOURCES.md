# Humboldt County Official Source Directory

Checked: 2026-07-18

This directory provides authoritative starting points for manual parcel research. It is not an automated connector catalog and it does not establish that a record is complete, current, legally effective, or associated with the correct parcel.

Humboldt County Planning & Building generally serves unincorporated areas. Incorporated cities maintain separate zoning, planning, building, and code-enforcement records. Confirm jurisdiction before relying on a county result.

## Parcel identity and assessment

### Humboldt County Property Assessment Inquiry

- URL: https://humboldtgov.org/230/Property-Assessment-Inquiry
- Use for: assessment number, fee parcel number, street-address search, assessment details, and assessor parcel-map starting points.
- Capture: query used, APN/assessment number, result date, displayed situs address, and any map reference.
- Limitation: assessor characteristics are maintained for assessment purposes and may contain errors, omissions, approximations, or update delays.

### Humboldt County Assessor

- URL: https://humboldtgov.org/220/Assessor
- Use for: office guidance, forms, assessment questions, value reviews, exemptions, and links to current assessor services.

## Recorded documents and ownership evidence

### Humboldt County Clerk-Recorder

- URL: https://humboldtgov.org/244/Clerk-Recorder
- Use for: recorder responsibilities, fee schedules, office access, and obtaining recorded documents or maps.
- Capture: instrument number, recording date, document type, grantor/grantee names as indexed, and the legal description shown on the document.
- Limitation: an assessor ownership display is not a substitute for reviewing the vesting deed and relevant later instruments. Online availability may not include every document or image needed for a complete chain.

## Zoning, land use, overlays, and parcel mapping

### Humboldt County Web GIS

- URL: https://humboldtgov.org/GIS
- About and disclaimer: https://humboldtgov.org/278/About-The-Web-GIS
- Use for: planning-level parcel, zoning, general-plan, jurisdiction, road, stream, aerial, hazard, and other map layers.
- Capture: layer names, map date when shown, parcel query, screenshot/export date, and any linked authoritative record.
- Limitation: county GIS expressly describes its information as planning data, warns that accuracy varies, and says it should not be relied on to determine legal boundaries or used for project design. Verify material conclusions with authoritative records and qualified professionals.

### Humboldt County Planning & Building

- URL: https://humboldtgov.org/2546/Planning-Building
- Use for: planning and building divisions, Accela access, county code, permit guidance, major projects, and jurisdiction-specific contacts.
- Limitation: county zoning and land-use services generally cover unincorporated areas. Contact the relevant city for incorporated parcels.

## Permits and inspections

### Humboldt County Building Inspection

- URL: https://humboldtgov.org/153/Building-Inspection
- Use for: Accela, permit-process guidance, inspection resources, building-code references, public-record-request links, and Safe Home Program information.

### First Time Applying for a Building Permit

- URL: https://humboldtgov.org/190/First-Time-Applying-for-a-Building-Permi
- Use for: current digital permit workflow, required documents, inspections, certificates of occupancy, and the relationship between permits and code-enforcement cases.

For an existing parcel, distinguish at least:

- application submitted;
- permit issued;
- revisions or conditions;
- inspections performed;
- final approval or certificate of occupancy; and
- expired, void, withdrawn, or unresolved permits.

A permit search result alone does not prove that work was completed or legally finaled.

## Code enforcement

### Humboldt County Code Enforcement

- URL: https://humboldtgov.org/2447/Code-Enforcement
- Use for: division scope and current contact information.

### County public records guidance

- URL: https://humboldtgov.org/395/Public-Information
- General public-records portal: https://humboldtgov.org/1309/Public-Records
- Use for: focused requests for existing county records when portal searches do not provide the needed notices, inspection records, orders, correspondence, hearing records, abatement records, lien records, or closure evidence.
- Limitation: a public-records request does not require an agency to create a record that does not exist. Define date ranges, parcel identifiers, departments, record types, and known case numbers narrowly.

## Property taxes and tax-default research

### View or Pay Property Taxes Online

- URL: https://humboldtgov.org/282/View-or-Pay-Property-Taxes-Online
- Use for: viewing tax bills and current payment information through the county's linked service.
- Capture: APN/assessment number, tax year, installment status, default references, and retrieval date.

### Property Tax Information

- URL: https://humboldtgov.org/2088/Property-Tax-Information
- Use for: secured, unsecured, supplemental, delinquency, default, and power-to-sell process explanations.
- Limitation: distinguish a delinquent installment, tax default, recorded notice of power to sell, scheduled auction, completed sale, redemption, rescission, and excess-proceeds process. They are not interchangeable events.

## Wells, septic, and environmental-health land-use records

### Humboldt County Environmental Health

- URL: https://humboldtgov.org/564/Environmental-Health
- Land Use Program: https://humboldtgov.org/685/Land-Use-Program
- Use for: onsite wastewater systems, wells and water systems, land-use review, hazardous-materials programs, and current contacts.
- Capture: permit or application number, system type, approval status, site evaluation, installation/final dates, and any operating or maintenance conditions.
- Limitation: the existence of a permit or application does not prove current system condition, capacity, legal suitability for a proposed use, or water quantity/quality.

## Research protocol

For every evidence item:

1. Confirm the parcel identifier and jurisdiction before searching.
2. Record the responsible agency or custodian.
3. Preserve the official URL or instrument/case/permit reference.
4. Record the date displayed on the source and the date retrieved.
5. State exactly what the source proves and what it does not prove.
6. Note conflicts between systems instead of choosing one silently.
7. Request the underlying record when a portal summary is insufficient.
8. Re-check time-sensitive information immediately before a transaction or filing.

Do not mark an item verified merely because a search returned no result. A no-result response may mean no record, a different index format, incomplete online coverage, incorrect identifiers, a different jurisdiction, or a record available only by request or in person.
