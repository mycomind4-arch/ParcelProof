# Privacy and Limitations

## Browser-local storage

ParcelProof stores project JSON in the browser's local storage for the website origin running the application. Version 0.2 does not transmit project contents to an application server because it has no application server.

Browser-local does not mean confidential or permanent:

- another person using the same unlocked browser profile may see stored projects;
- browser extensions and compromised devices may access page data;
- browser cleanup, private-browsing rules, profile resets, device loss, or storage quotas may erase projects;
- browser synchronization or device backups may copy browser data outside the operator's direct control; and
- moving the application to a different domain creates a different storage origin and does not migrate projects.

Export important projects regularly and store exported JSON and reports in a location appropriate for their sensitivity.

## Appropriate content

Use ParcelProof for public records and information the user is authorized to collect and review. Minimize personal information. Record the source and conclusion needed for due diligence instead of copying unnecessary personal details.

Do not use a public or shared deployment for:

- Social Security numbers, driver's-license images, identity documents, or financial-account data;
- protected health information;
- privileged legal communications;
- sealed, confidential, juvenile, child, or criminal-justice records;
- authentication credentials or access tokens; or
- any material whose storage requires contractual, statutory, or regulatory controls not provided by a static browser application.

## Evidence limitations

A verified status means the user marked an item verified and supplied the required provenance fields. It does not mean ParcelProof independently authenticated the record or determined that the underlying agency data is complete, current, legally effective, or associated with the correct parcel.

Government databases may contain delays, indexing differences, scanning defects, incomplete historical coverage, redactions, superseded records, or jurisdictional gaps. Parcel identifiers, street addresses, ownership names, and legal descriptions can change or be represented differently across systems.

## Professional-review boundary

ParcelProof is an organizational and decision-support tool. It does not provide or replace:

- a title search, title insurance commitment, or legal opinion;
- a boundary, easement, access, or topographic survey;
- legal advice about ownership, liens, enforcement, due process, or property rights;
- planning, zoning, building-code, engineering, geotechnical, wildfire, flood, coastal, biological, or environmental clearance;
- appraisal, lending, insurance, or tax advice; or
- an on-site inspection.

High-consequence decisions should be reviewed by the relevant agency and appropriately licensed professionals.

## Operator responsibilities

The operator should define the review scope, identify the jurisdiction, preserve dated source citations, distinguish facts from reviewer interpretation, document unavailable records, and disclose the limitations of the delivered report.
